# Shamba Connected Futures

# SHAMBA RECORDS — COMPLETE CORPORATE WEBSITE REDESIGN

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Redesign the existing Shamba Records website into a premium, modern African technology and financial innovation company website.

This is a complete brand positioning and website experience upgrade, not just a visual refresh.

Shamba Records should be presented as a technology-driven company building digital infrastructure, financial solutions, intelligent systems and connected ecosystems that empower businesses, institutions, organisations and communities.

The company should no longer be perceived as a platform that exclusively serves individual farmers.

Its broader identity should communicate:

- Technology and software innovation.
- Financial technology (Fintech).
- Digital financial infrastructure.
- Artificial intelligence and data intelligence.
- Agricultural technology and enterprise solutions.
- Digital platforms and business integrations.
- Blockchain and traceability.
- Financial inclusion and connected ecosystems.

The website must communicate that Shamba Records builds technology that connects different players, industries and opportunities.

The design should remain relevant for the next 10–15 years. Avoid temporary design trends, excessive gradients, unnecessary animations and generic template-style layouts.

IMPORTANT: The existing Shamba Records homepage already has a hero section that must be used as the primary design reference.

DO NOT replace the hero with a completely different layout.

Study the existing hero section and preserve its fundamental visual composition:

Maintain the same visual identity and composition that make the existing hero recognisable.

- Refine the circular image arrangement.

The hero must continue to look recognisably like the existing Shamba Records hero.

Do not redesign its fundamental layout.

This requirement takes priority over any alternative hero design suggestions.

# 3. BRAND IDENTITY AND DESIGN SYSTEM

Establish one consistent and permanent visual identity across the entire website.

### Primary brand colour

Shamba Evergreen: #087A55

This must be the dominant and recognisable brand colour.

### Supporting palette

- Deep Ink: #14231D
- Soft Ivory: #F4F8F5
- Soft Sage: #B9DCCB
- Pure White: #FFFFFF
- Secondary Text: #66756B

Use these colours consistently.

Colour rules:
- #087A55 for primary buttons, active states, important highlights, icons and brand accents.
- #14231D for headings and important text.
- #F4F8F5 for soft backgrounds and alternating sections.
- #B9DCCB for subtle decorative elements and illustrations.
- White for spacious, clean content sections.

Do not introduce unrelated dominant colours.

Avoid making every section green. Use a thoughtful balance of white, ivory, dark ink and evergreen.

### Typography

Use Manrope or Inter.

Typography should have:
- Strong, large headlines.
- Clear heading hierarchy.
- Comfortable paragraph readability.
- Modern font weights.
- Consistent spacing.

### General design language

The overall aesthetic should be:

- Premium.
- Minimalist.
- Corporate.
- Technology-driven.
- Trustworthy.
- Innovative.
- African-inspired without relying on stereotypical African patterns.
- Professional enough for banks, financial institutions, investors and enterprise partners.

Use large whitespace, balanced grids, elegant cards, subtle borders, rounded corners and carefully selected photography.

Avoid excessive shadows, glassmorphism, neon effects, unnecessary decorative icons and crowded sections.
